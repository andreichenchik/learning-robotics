Pose and differential drive

A minimal geometric model of a two-wheeled robot on a plane.

Pose

q = (x, y, θ)

x,y locate the midpoint of the wheel axle in the world frame. θ gives the forward direction.

Discrete kinematics

xₖ₊₁ = xₖ + Δt · vₖ cos θₖ
yₖ₊₁ = yₖ + Δt · vₖ sin θₖ
θₖ₊₁ = wrap(θₖ + Δt · ωₖ)

v is forward velocity and ω is angular velocity. There is no independent lateral command.

From chassis to wheels

φ̇L = (v − Lω/2) / r
φ̇R = (v + Lω/2) / r

r is wheel radius and L is the distance between the wheels.

v ≠ 0, ω = 0Straight line.
v = 0, ω ≠ 0Rotation in place.
v ≠ 0, ω ≠ 0Arc.
Model boundary. The kinematics assume no lateral slip and instant execution of velocity commands. Motors, mass, traction, and odometry error will appear later.
