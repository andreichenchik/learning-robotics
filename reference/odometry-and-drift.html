Odometry and drift

Estimate differential-drive pose from incremental wheel motion.

Measurement

ΔsL = r · ΔφL
ΔsR = r · ΔφR

Convert encoder rotation increments into left and right wheel travel.

Chassis increment

Δs = (ΔsR + ΔsL) / 2
Δθ = (ΔsR − ΔsL) / L

The average creates forward motion; the difference creates rotation.

Midpoint integration

θmid = θ̂ + Δθ/2
x̂' = x̂ + Δs cos θmid
ŷ' = ŷ + Δs sin θmid
θ̂' = wrap(θ̂ + Δθ)
BiasPersistent error with a consistent direction.
NoiseSample-to-sample measurement variation.
DriftAccumulated pose error after repeated integration.
Remember. Odometry estimates change in pose; it does not observe absolute pose. External measurements are needed to correct accumulated drift.
