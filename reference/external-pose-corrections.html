External pose corrections

Combine a drifting prediction with an occasional world-referenced measurement.

Predict

q̂⁻ ← odometry_update(q̂, ΔsL, ΔsR)

The minus superscript marks the estimate before the new external measurement is applied.

Compute the innovation

ex = zx − x̂⁻
ey = zy − ŷ⁻
eθ = wrap(zθ − θ̂⁻)

The innovation is the discrepancy between the absolute measurement z and the prediction.

Correct

x̂⁺ = x̂⁻ + αex
ŷ⁺ = ŷ⁻ + αey
θ̂⁺ = wrap(θ̂⁻ + αeθ)
α = 0Pure odometry; drift remains.
0 < α < 1Partial correction; smoothness–drift tradeoff.
α = 1Pure measurement at every correction.
Model boundary. A fixed weight does not represent changing uncertainty, latency, outliers, or correlations. It prepares the predict–correct structure; a Kalman filter computes its gain from uncertainty.
