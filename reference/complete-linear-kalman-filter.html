Complete linear Kalman filter

Carry one corrected Gaussian belief through repeated motion predictions and measurement corrections.

Stored belief

x̂⁺ = estimated positionestimated velocity P⁺ = covariance of its error

The corrected pair (x̂⁺,P⁺) is the filter's recursive memory.

Prediction

x̂⁻ = F x̂⁺
P⁻ = F P⁺ Fᵀ + Q
FMoves the state through the known model.
F P FᵀPropagates existing uncertainty.
QAdds fresh process uncertainty.

Measurement correction

y = z − Hx̂⁻
S = HP⁻Hᵀ + R
K = P⁻HᵀS⁻¹
x̂⁺ = x̂⁻ + Ky
P⁺ = (I − KH)P⁻
HMaps state into sensor space.
RDescribes measurement-error covariance.
KMaps innovation back into state correction.

Position–velocity with position GPS

F = 1Δt01 H = 10

The velocity component of the gain is Kv=P⁻pv/S. A position measurement corrects velocity only when position and velocity errors are coupled.

Missing measurement. Keep the prediction as the current belief: x̂⁺←x̂⁻ and P⁺←P⁻. Correct when a measurement becomes available.
Model boundary. Exact optimality requires linear motion and measurement models with correctly modeled Gaussian noise. Validate outliers and tune Q and R from evidence.
