EKF pose localization

Carry one local Gaussian belief over planar pose through nonlinear odometry and occasional world-frame pose measurements.

Stored belief

x̂ = xyθ P = covariance of pose error

The corrected pair (x̂⁺,P⁺) is the recursive memory. It summarizes previous actions and observations.

Nonlinear odometry prediction

φ = θ + Δθ/2
x′ = x + d cos φ
y′ = y + d sin φ
θ′ = wrap(θ + Δθ)
x̂⁻ = f(x̂⁺,u)
P⁻ = F P⁺ Fᵀ + Q
F = ∂f/∂x = 10−d sin φ01d cos φ001
fMoves the mean through the nonlinear geometry.
FMoves nearby errors through the local geometry.
QAdds uncertainty from imperfect motion.

World-frame pose correction

For a camera that directly reports z=(zx,zy,zθ), the observation model is H=I:

y = z − x̂⁻; wrap yθ
S = P⁻ + R
K = P⁻S⁻¹
x̂⁺ = x̂⁻ + Ky; wrap θ̂⁺
P⁺ = (I − K)P⁻
RCamera pose-error covariance.
KTrust by state direction.
P⁺Reduced local uncertainty.

Physical reading of covariance

  • Pxx, Pyy, Pθθ: marginal error variances for the pose coordinates.
  • Pxθ, Pyθ: coupling between position and heading errors.
  • The top-left 2×2 block produces the position covariance ellipse.
  • After motion, heading uncertainty spreads into position through the third column of F.
Always wrap angular differences. Compute heading innovation with the shortest signed difference, then wrap the corrected heading again.

When this model is appropriate

Use itRough initial pose, locally smooth motion, approximately Gaussian errors.
Validate itOutliers, latency, biases, and evidence for chosen Q and R.
Change methodSeveral distant pose hypotheses or an unknown map.
